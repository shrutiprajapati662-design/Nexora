# Nexora

## Project Overview

Nexora is a production-level AI-powered career platform built on the MERN stack. It provides an all-in-one ecosystem where job seekers, recruiters, companies, and administrators can interact through a secure, scalable, and intelligent web application.

The platform is designed to simplify the complete hiring journey, including resume creation, job discovery, applications, recruitment management, interview preparation, AI-powered career guidance, and professional growth.

Rather than functioning as a traditional job portal, Nexora aims to become a complete career management platform powered by modern technologies and artificial intelligence.

---

# Vision

To build one of the most intelligent, secure, and user-friendly career platforms that empowers individuals to grow professionally while helping recruiters and companies hire better talent.
